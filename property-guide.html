How to Compare Properties in NCR

Use this guide when shortlisting a home, rental or commercial property in Noida, Greater Noida, Greater Noida West, Ghaziabad, Meerut or the Yamuna Expressway area.

Start with your location and total budget

Choose a city on the Buy or Rent page, then narrow the list by society or locality, property type, BHK and budget. Visit the locality at the times you normally travel. Compare the actual route to work, school and everyday services before deciding.

Compare like-for-like property details

Ask what the advertised area measures: carpet area, built-up area or another measurement. Check the layout, floor, lift access, parking, furnishing and the condition of the property during a visit. A missing field or photo means the detail has not been provided; it is not confirmation that a feature is included.

Questions for a purchase enquiry

  • Is the asking price current, and which additional charges apply?
  • Is the property occupied, vacant or under construction?
  • Which ownership and project documents are available for independent review?
  • What is the proposed possession date, and what has been agreed in writing?

Questions for a rental enquiry

  • What are the monthly rent, maintenance charge and security deposit?
  • Which fittings, appliances and parking spaces are included?
  • What are the move-in date, notice period and repair responsibilities?
  • Are there society move-in charges or restrictions relevant to your household?

Use photos to prepare for a visit

Ask for recent photos of the rooms, kitchen, bathrooms and building entrance. Photos help with shortlisting but do not replace a visit. Confirm the exact address with RealtyAdda: the map link on a listing searches its stated locality.

Before committing

Confirm availability, the full cost and written terms directly. Arrange independent professional review of relevant documents before making a commitment. A listing on RealtyAdda is not a certification of title, approvals or condition.

Posting your property

Provide the correct city and locality, property type, price, area and a useful description. Upload clear photos you have permission to use. Submissions are reviewed before publication; submission does not mean that a property is already live.
